# Different stories for the same frontend

## A surface can propose a different activity

Imagine the product as a notebook, a control room, or a conversation. A notebook makes incomplete thoughts and history useful; a control room emphasizes current exceptions; a conversation emphasizes sequence and response. These metaphors can produce genuinely different journeys rather than cosmetic variants.

## Change the experience of time

What would the journey mean if responses were instant, connectivity intermittent, or a job took an hour? Consider continuity, retained intent, and recoverable interruption. A spinner is one representation of waiting, not the definition of it.

## Example

A reporting dashboard can become saved questions with annotations, a stream of exceptions, or a periodic narrative addressed to a team. Each direction changes what is worth caching, sharing, and keeping in the URL. Explore the experience before treating a rendering strategy as the answer.

## Follow a promising thread

State ownership, consistency, and interaction contracts can be investigated in the neighbouring notes when they illuminate a chosen idea. No list of dimensions has to be completed. The useful result may be an alternative journey or a sharper question for the product owner.
