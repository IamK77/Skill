# Navigate by the experience being proposed

## An opening

Retell the same product as a document, a conversation, and a workbench. Use the routing questions to explore how navigation, persistence, and rendering change under each interpretation. No branch needs to win until the human wants to pursue a particular journey.

## Elaborate a candidate

Use the current uncertainty to select a thread: navigation, authorship, time, information structure, or integration. Do not make every frontend question begin with a complete architecture audit. Ordinary CSS or syntax questions can be answered directly, while an unsettled journey can remain exploratory.

## A small comparison

A report may be a page to read, a job to revisit, or a shared workspace to manipulate. Sketch the consequences of each for navigation and recovery. Keel can build one selected journey later; there is no prerequisite certificate for exploring it.

## Keep the choice open

Use this note when its question is useful, not as a required inspection. A sketch, a contrast, or an unresolved question can be the result. The [parent lens](../SKILL.md) offers other ways into the subject; execution begins only when a direction and task are chosen.
