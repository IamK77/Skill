# What does the surface promise during uncertainty?

## An opening

Change what the interface promises during uncertainty: immediate local acknowledgement, visible pending work, or a delayed but final answer. Try the same failure under each promise. The perceived contract can generate a different async model instead of merely a different loading animation.

## Elaborate a candidate

Distinguish immediate feedback, local intent, pending remote work, and confirmed outcome where they imply different choices. Consider interruption, outdated responses, and recovery as meanings of the experience rather than only implementation edge cases. A fast acknowledgment need not claim durable success.

## A small comparison

Compare a save control that confirms only after persistence with one that records a visible pending draft. Both can be coherent if the remaining uncertainty is clear and recovery is supported. The choice can generate a different interaction and state architecture.
