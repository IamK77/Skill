# Authorship and continuity in a frontend

## An opening

Treat a workspace as a shared live surface, a personal draft, and a published snapshot. The same data can carry different authorship and time semantics in each. Explore what sharing or refresh should mean before choosing where the state lives.

## Elaborate a candidate

Identify who owns a value, what moment a displayed copy describes, and what refresh, navigation, or collaboration should mean. A URL, local draft, server record, and shared live surface can legitimately hold related representations with different responsibilities. Make the synchronisation or reconciliation promise explicit for a chosen design.

## A small comparison

A personal draft and a published snapshot can coexist without one being an error. Compare that model with immediate shared editing and inspect what conflict or back/forward navigation would mean. The distinction is a product experience as well as a storage choice.

Use this note when its question is useful, not as a required inspection. A sketch, a contrast, or an unresolved question can be the result. The [parent lens](../SKILL.md) offers other ways into the subject; execution begins only when a direction and task are chosen.
