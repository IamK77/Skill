# A visual voice across several dimensions

## Borrow a relationship, not a costume

A field notebook offers annotation and accumulated traces. A musical score offers rhythm and simultaneous voices. An instrument panel offers concentrated signal and calibrated attention. Ask which relationship belongs to the product before copying any familiar palette or typeface.

## Compose a tension

Try warmth with precision, density with calm, or authority with play. Let different dimensions carry different halves: restrained colour with lively imagery, compact layout with patient motion, or precise numerals with conversational text. Coherence need not mean uniformity.

## Example

For a tide-monitoring tool, a chart metaphor suggests layered geographic information; an instrument metaphor suggests urgent measurements; a notebook metaphor suggests observations over time. Each could support a coherent direction. Combining all their visual signatures at once may obscure the useful distinction.

## Develop only what is interesting

The atelier lenses can deepen colour, type, form, layout, imagery, or motion independently. A selected direction can become reusable tokens and components through systems. Neither a universal visual audit nor a prior canon certificate is required.
