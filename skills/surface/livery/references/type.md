# Typography as a relationship with the reader

## An opening

Cast the same product text as a letter, a ledger, and an annotated field guide. Use actual paragraphs, labels, and numbers to discover the voice. Font family count and modular ratios are implementation choices, not prerequisites for a typographic point of view.

## Elaborate a candidate

Use actual text and data to explore hierarchy, measure, spacing, weight, and font features. Compare the intended voice under supported languages, content lengths, fallback, and resizing conditions. A typeface or modular ratio can support a direction but cannot certify it.

## A small comparison

The same financial summary can feel like a compact ledger or a deliberate explanatory letter. Render real numerals, headings, and paragraphs in both. Preserve whichever relationship suits the selected activity; a third family or an optical exception is not automatically an error.

## Keep the choice open

Use this note when its question is useful, not as a required inspection. A sketch, a contrast, or an unresolved question can be the result. The [parent lens](../SKILL.md) offers other ways into the subject; execution begins only when a direction and task are chosen.
