# A visual grammar rather than a collection of assets

## An opening

Change the imagery from objects to relationships. Instead of illustrating a “secure” product with a shield, try paths, permissions, partitions, or custody. A family of images can emerge from a shared construction process, while labels preserve meanings that a metaphor cannot carry alone.

## Elaborate a candidate

Choose what the image or mark should help someone perceive, then define a construction rule that can generate a family. Consider metaphor, viewpoint, stroke or material, labels, and alternative content. Check rights and actual asset behaviour before implementation; expressive similarity is not a substitute for permission or meaning.

## A small comparison

A status family can express progress through growth, assembly, or changing paths instead of unrelated checkmarks and illustrations. Compare the same important states and preserve explicit language where the metaphor alone would be ambiguous.
