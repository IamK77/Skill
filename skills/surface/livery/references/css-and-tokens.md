# Translate a selected relationship into reusable implementation

## An opening

Hold the content constant and try a visual direction driven by typography, one driven by surfaces, and one driven by spatial rhythm. Only after a direction becomes useful, identify which relationships deserve reusable tokens. Implementation constraints can refine an idea without becoming the source of every creative choice.

## Elaborate a candidate

Use existing layout and styling conventions where they support the idea. Identify recurring semantic roles before extracting tokens and keep component-specific or optical exceptions visible. Avoid treating a token scale, utility framework, or stylesheet organization as the source of a visual point of view.

## A small comparison

Apply one direction to a paragraph, a form, and a data view. Extract the relationships that actually recur, then inspect whether the implementation preserves them under content and viewport changes. Systems can formalize the reusable contract when adoption is wanted.

## Keep the choice open

Use this note when its question is useful, not as a required inspection. A sketch, a contrast, or an unresolved question can be the result. The [parent lens](../SKILL.md) offers other ways into the subject; execution begins only when a direction and task are chosen.
