# Check the selected journey under its actual constraints

## Use when

Use the supported users, input modes, devices, content, and adopted accessibility/performance requirements.

## Method

Exercise semantic structure, names and states, keyboard paths, focus behaviour, resizing/reflow, and relevant nonvisual information. Measure the performance of representative interactions under stated conditions. Combine automated observations with manual checks that reach behaviour automation may miss, and retain regressions for important failures.

## Example and record

A modal can look correct and pass a limited scan while focus escapes or does not return appropriately. Exercise the keyboard journey and interruption paths. A fast first render does not establish responsive interaction after a large data update.

## Limits and handoff

Report the specific checks and environments, not blanket compliance or universal speed. If a requirement is unclear, ask its owner rather than inventing a legal or technical baseline.
