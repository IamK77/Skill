# Treat popularity and polish as leads, not verdicts

## Use when

Use a project claim and the practical consequence if it is wrong for the intended use.

## Method

Ask what observation would directly support the claim: reachable implementation, a meaningful test, a reproducible consumer example, or an attributable maintenance decision. Use stars, downloads, presentation, and activity as context for where to look, then inspect the relevant substance. Keep unsupported claims unknown.

## Example and record

A repository can have frequent automated commits while a critical compatibility issue remains unresolved. Another may change rarely because its scope is stable. Inspect the issue, consumers, and supported contract before assigning maintenance risk.

## Limits and handoff

Signals can mislead without deliberate deception. Avoid inferring dishonesty or capability from appearances, and do not present this assessment skill as the retired root A/B experiment platform.
