# Capture a shortlist with evidence and uncertainty

## Use when

Use the stated software need, constraints, search budget, and candidate sources.

## Method

Apply the inclusion and exclusion criteria consistently enough to avoid padding the list. Inspect primary project material for the capability that matters, record a revision or access date, and capture why each candidate remains plausible. Keep disqualifying uncertainties and unavailable evidence alongside favourable observations.

## Example and record

A repository may advertise a feature while its documentation describes only a planned extension. Capture that distinction rather than listing the feature as implemented. Return a bounded shortlist or a useful negative result when no candidate fits.

## Limits and handoff

Discovery is not adoption approval. Popularity and attractive documentation may help locate candidates but do not establish maintenance quality, security, licence fit, or compatibility.
