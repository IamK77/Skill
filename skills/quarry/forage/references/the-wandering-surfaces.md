# Follow useful leads across different discovery surfaces

## Use when

Use a concrete problem and a bounded allowance for exploratory search.

## Method

Consult relevant package/code indexes, dependency graphs, integration documentation, technical discussions, and neighbouring projects. Follow a lead when it reveals a mechanism, maintained implementation, or alternate vocabulary related to the need. Record the primary source behind a recommendation rather than treating a listicle or repeated mention as independent evidence.

## Example and record

A maintained adapter may reveal the underlying library actually implementing the desired capability. Inspect both responsibilities before shortlisting the adapter as a complete solution. A discussion can also reveal an unresolved limitation worth recording.

## Limits and handoff

Wandering should expand the candidate space, not silently replace the requirement. Return to the stated constraints and stop when the search budget no longer justifies another branch.
