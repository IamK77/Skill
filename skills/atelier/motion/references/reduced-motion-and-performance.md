# Preserve information when motion changes

## An opening

Create a still version of the interaction that preserves its information, then compare it with an animated version. The difference can reveal whether motion contributes continuity, emphasis, or merely noise. Reduced-motion preferences and measured device performance become implementation requirements for the chosen candidate.

## Elaborate a candidate

Develop a meaningful still or reduced-motion alternative rather than merely shortening every duration. Treat motion preferences and the product's accessibility requirements as part of the implemented contract. Measure real interactions on relevant devices; available frame time depends on refresh rate and competes with other work, so a single universal millisecond budget is misleading.

## A small comparison

Replace a spatial sweep that connects source and destination with a clear still state or another non-motion cue. Test interruption and repeated actions as well as the ideal transition. A smooth animation does not establish that the underlying operation succeeded.

## Keep the choice open

Use this note when its question is useful, not as a required inspection. A sketch, a contrast, or an unresolved question can be the result. The [parent lens](../SKILL.md) offers other ways into the subject; execution begins only when a direction and task are chosen.
