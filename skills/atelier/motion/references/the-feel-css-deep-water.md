# Coordinate timing without decorating every event

## An opening

Move emphasis away from the middle of an animation: try anticipation, a brief hold, or an unusually quiet ending. Experiment with how several small transitions share time. The expressive result may come from removing movement rather than adding another effect.

## Elaborate a candidate

Separate the event's meaning from the property being animated. Consider sequencing, overlap, easing, and the quiet before or after movement. Test retargeting, cancellation, layout effects, and repeated interactions in the actual rendering environment. Prefer the simplest technique that expresses the selected relationship.

## A small comparison

Try a notification that appears immediately and settles quietly, then a version that holds before disappearing. Compare whether the timing makes the information easier to perceive or simply delays the work. Keep the still alternative and relevant preference handling available.

## Keep the choice open

Use this note when its question is useful, not as a required inspection. A sketch, a contrast, or an unresolved question can be the result. The [parent lens](../SKILL.md) offers other ways into the subject; execution begins only when a direction and task are chosen.
