# A visual family from a construction process

## An opening

Represent a subject literally, as a diagram of its relationships, and as an abstract process. A network product need not begin with shields: paths, partitions, or woven boundaries may reveal a different story. Explore a family of images through shared construction rather than one repeated decorative motif.

## Elaborate a candidate

Choose the role of the image: explanation, orientation, emotional character, evidence, or invitation. Define a small set of relationships that can generate several images, such as layering, line direction, texture, or variation. Keep source rights, alternative content, resolution, and loading behaviour in view when selecting actual assets.

## A small comparison

Develop three illustrations for different states of the same activity using one construction rule. Compare that family with literal imagery. The useful criterion is whether the relationship helps the subject become perceivable, not whether every image shares the same fashionable style label.

## Keep the choice open

Use this note when its question is useful, not as a required inspection. A sketch, a contrast, or an unresolved question can be the result. The [parent lens](../SKILL.md) offers other ways into the subject; execution begins only when a direction and task are chosen.
