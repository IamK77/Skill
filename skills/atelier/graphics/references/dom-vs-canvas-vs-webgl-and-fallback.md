# Choose a medium for the relationship

## An opening

Imagine the same idea as readable annotations, a manipulable diagram, and an ambient generative field. Different representations invite different interaction and rendering choices. Preserve the essential meaning in a still or nonvisual form; the most powerful renderer is not necessarily the most useful medium.

## Elaborate a candidate

DOM and SVG can suit structured, labelled, or individually interactive content. Canvas can suit a custom drawn surface, while GPU techniques may fit particular large or dynamic visual workloads. These are tendencies to investigate, not a universal ordering. Consider semantics, interaction, text, selection, accessibility, performance, and maintenance together.

## A small comparison

Prototype a labelled graph with real content and the intended interaction. Compare a structured representation with a drawn surface rather than benchmarking empty shapes alone. A still or textual alternative should preserve useful information when the interactive or GPU path is unavailable.

## Keep the choice open

Use this note when its question is useful, not as a required inspection. A sketch, a contrast, or an unresolved question can be the result. The [parent lens](../SKILL.md) offers other ways into the subject; execution begins only when a direction and task are chosen.
