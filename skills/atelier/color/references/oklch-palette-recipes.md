# Palette recipes as experiments

## An opening

Try a palette as a family of related pigments, then as contrasting voices. In the first, neighbours may share temperature and vary gently; in the second, selected roles may deliberately differ. A numerical recipe can reproduce one candidate without making every semantic role or theme follow the same formula.

## Elaborate a candidate

Choose semantic roles first, then vary lightness, chroma, and hue to explore a family. Keep track of where a chosen coordinate lies outside the target display gamut and inspect the rendered result. Semantic roles may need different relationships in different themes; an evenly spaced numeric ramp does not automatically produce evenly useful interface states.

## A small comparison

Build a small specimen containing real text, a border, a status indicator, a selected state, and a large background. Change one relationship at a time, then compare a coherent alternative. Record the chosen values as a reproducible candidate and measure applicable contrast requirements separately.

## Keep the choice open

Use this note when its question is useful, not as a required inspection. A sketch, a contrast, or an unresolved question can be the result. The [parent lens](../SKILL.md) offers other ways into the subject; execution begins only when a direction and task are chosen.
