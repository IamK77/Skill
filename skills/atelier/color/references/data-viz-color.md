# Colour that matches the data relationship

## An opening

Tell the data story once through identity, once through magnitude, and once through departure from a meaningful reference. A set of categories, an ordered ramp, and a diverging relationship suggest different questions the reader can ask. Let the data meaning, not an attractive rainbow, motivate the experiment.

## Elaborate a candidate

Distinguish category identity, ordered magnitude, and divergence around a meaningful reference. An ordered ramp can use a consistent lightness progression; categorical colours need useful distinctions rather than an implied rank. A diverging scheme should have a justified centre. Position, direct labels, shape, and pattern can carry information that colour alone should not bear.

## A small comparison

Compare the same small dataset with a sequential ramp, a categorical palette, and direct labels. The inappropriate mappings should reveal why the data relation matters. A familiar rainbow can obscure some ordered quantities, but no palette family is universally forbidden regardless of the variable it represents.

## Keep the choice open

Use this note when its question is useful, not as a required inspection. A sketch, a contrast, or an unresolved question can be the result. The [parent lens](../SKILL.md) offers other ways into the subject; execution begins only when a direction and task are chosen.
