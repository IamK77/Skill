# A dark theme as a separate mapping

## An opening

Imagine the same product as a dim room with illuminated objects, then as luminous marks in open darkness. These produce different surface and emphasis relationships. A night theme can be a distinct interpretation of the activity rather than an inverted daytime palette.

## Elaborate a candidate

Preserve the meaning of semantic roles while allowing their actual values to change between themes. Compare surface separation, emphasis, images, shadows, and interaction states in the rendered candidate. Dark does not simply mean subtracting every light-theme channel from its maximum; nor must every elevated surface follow one fixed brightness formula.

## A small comparison

Use a real screen containing text, a selected item, a disabled control, an image, and an overlay. Check whether their relationships survive the new mapping. A theme can be visually distinct while preserving the task's information and interaction requirements.
