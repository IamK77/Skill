# Let difficult content shape the composition

## An opening

Compose with the long name, translated label, missing image, or unusually small viewport first. Treat the awkward case as a source of spatial ideas. A layout that grows from it may be more interesting than a perfect hero screen surrounded by patches.

## Elaborate a candidate

Try representative long labels, translated passages, missing images, small or large datasets, and narrow viewports. Decide where wrapping, reflow, disclosure, scrolling, or truncation preserves meaning. Avoid treating every awkward case as a bug in the content or relying on one ideal screenshot.

## A small comparison

Use an unusually long project name and a zero-result view to sketch two layouts. One may emphasise a stable column structure; another may let content determine the rhythm. Bring a selected candidate to implementation with its intended overflow and reading behaviour explicit.

## Keep the choice open

Use this note when its question is useful, not as a required inspection. A sketch, a contrast, or an unresolved question can be the result. The [parent lens](../SKILL.md) offers other ways into the subject; execution begins only when a direction and task are chosen.
