# A typographic voice across different text

## An opening

Begin with real passages in the scripts the product serves and compare their texture, emphasis, and line rhythm. Seek a shared voice rather than identical metrics. A direction discovered through one script may need a different structural expression in another.

## Elaborate a candidate

Use actual supported scripts and content instead of assuming one specimen represents every language. Compare glyph texture, punctuation, emphasis, line breaking, numerals, and fallback behaviour. A family resemblance can use different metrics or structural choices while preserving the same hierarchy and intent.

## A small comparison

Place equivalent short and long passages in a component with real controls and data. Inspect missing glyphs, changed wrapping, and the reading rhythm. A selected typographic system should acknowledge those differences rather than force every script into the same Latin-centric scale.

## Keep the choice open

Use this note when its question is useful, not as a required inspection. A sketch, a contrast, or an unresolved question can be the result. The [parent lens](../SKILL.md) offers other ways into the subject; execution begins only when a direction and task are chosen.
