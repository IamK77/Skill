# Prepare an attributable submission or response

## Use when

Use the current manuscript, author decisions, actual submission rules or reviewer comments, and authorised communication scope.

## Method

Verify the target requirements and prepare the requested package. For a response, answer the specific comment, identify evidence or manuscript changes, and distinguish new experiments from clarification. Keep unresolved author decisions visible and preserve a versioned record. Submit or contact others only with explicit authority.

## Example and record

If a reviewer asks about a missing failure regime, report the actual additional check or explain its limitation and narrow the claim. Do not invent a result or promise an experiment that was not authorised or feasible.

## Limits and handoff

Deadlines and response conventions come from the actual venue, not a universal 48-hour rule. Persistence can mean revising the question or choosing another venue; it does not mean concealing rejection reasons or inflating claims.
