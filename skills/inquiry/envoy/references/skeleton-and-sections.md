# Organise a manuscript around supported claims

## Use when

Use the selected contribution, evidence and limitations, audience, and actual venue requirements.

## Method

Outline the reader's question, the proposed contribution, the method, the evidence, and what remains unresolved. Use figures and tables where they make an argument inspectable rather than merely repeat prose. Keep methodological detail sufficient for the intended scrutiny and connect numeric statements to retained artifacts.

## Example and record

A result section should distinguish the primary comparison from exploratory follow-up and explain an important failure regime near the claim it limits. The limitation should not be hidden in an unrelated closing paragraph.

## Limits and handoff

There is no universal section order or page allocation for every field. Follow the actual target format while preserving a coherent argument; do not manufacture a missing experiment or citation to fill a template.
