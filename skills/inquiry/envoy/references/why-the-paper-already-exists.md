# Build communication from the research record

## Use when

Use the protocol, run manifest, analysis, claim set, and limitations already produced by the work.

## Method

Reuse those artifacts as evidence and structure, not as prose to paste blindly. Trace the argument from the reader's question through the selected method to the observed result. Resolve inconsistencies in numbers, definitions, and scope before polishing style. Keep authorship and interpretation decisions attributable.

## Example and record

The protocol may explain why a comparison was chosen, while a later deviation log explains why the implementation differed. A useful methods section reflects both rather than rewriting the plan as though the deviation had always been intended.

## Limits and handoff

Good provenance makes writing easier but does not mean the manuscript is already complete. Communication still needs synthesis, audience judgement, and author review; a checklist cannot supply those decisions.
