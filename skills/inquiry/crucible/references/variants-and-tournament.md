# Variants as different causal stories

## Vary the mechanism, not just the label

Try changing what is represented, what is remembered, who acts, how feedback arrives, or what objective is negotiated. Several parameter settings of the same method may be useful later, but they do not exhaust the idea space.

## Borrow and recombine

Combine the representation of one approach with the update rule of another. Invert an expensive assumption: perfect information becomes partial observation, stationary demand becomes adaptation, abundant compute becomes selective attention. Keep the simplest interesting case visible before adding a framework.

## Example

A search process repeatedly restarts after failure. A memory story retains regions to avoid; a market story reallocates effort by opportunity; a developmental story changes the representation over time. Each proposes a different causal explanation for progress.

## No compulsory tournament

Sketch how alternatives might behave in one revealing situation. A contrast can improve the ideas even if no winner is selected. If a human requests an experiment, ledger can define fair comparisons and budgets. Do not force speculative mechanisms into a ranking merely to declare this exploration complete.
