# Test an explanation rather than only an outcome

## Use when

Use a proposed causal or algorithmic explanation and observations that could distinguish it from alternatives.

## Method

Identify the component, information path, or resource change the explanation relies on. Compare a removal, substitution, controlled perturbation, or intermediate observation that addresses that relation. Account for changes in compute, data, and tuning that might explain the outcome instead. Keep empirical support distinct from proof of a unique mechanism.

## Example and record

Removing a memory component may also reduce model capacity and runtime. A simpler substitution or matched-resource comparison can clarify what the result actually supports. Record those limits rather than declaring the original narrative proven by one ablation.

## Limits and handoff

Mechanism probes can be inconclusive or suggest a new explanation. Preserve that result and narrow the claim; do not require every useful empirical finding to have a complete theoretical account.
