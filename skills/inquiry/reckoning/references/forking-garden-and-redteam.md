# Expose analytic choices and alternative explanations

## Use when

Use the original plan, actual deviations, and the sequence of exploratory analyses.

## Method

List material choices that could change the conclusion: exclusions, aggregation, metrics, subgroups, stopping, and comparison families. Check plausible alternatives and keep their results attributable. Distinguish sensitivity analysis from a post-hoc attempt to rescue a preferred claim. Preserve negative and ambiguous findings.

## Example and record

A method may look favourable only after dropping a difficult instance class. Inspect the reason for exclusion and compare the stated population before narrowing the conclusion. An exploratory subgroup result may motivate another experiment, but it should not be presented as a preregistered discovery.

## Limits and handoff

Exploration is legitimate when labelled accurately. An adversarial review need not become a ritual or a fixed-duration tournament; focus on choices that materially affect the inference.
