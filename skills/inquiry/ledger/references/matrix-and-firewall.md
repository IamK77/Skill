# Connect a chosen claim to planned evidence

## Use when

Use the hypothesis selected for a committed experiment and the observations that could distinguish it from alternatives.

## Method

Map each principal claim to a comparison, outcome measure, and conditions under which the interpretation would narrow or change. Record the plan version before the committed evaluation. Keep exploratory observations labelled as exploratory and record later deviations rather than rewriting the original plan to match a result.

## Example and record

A scaling claim may need a curve across sizes and resource costs, while a mechanism claim needs a comparison that changes the proposed mechanism. Unexpected exploratory findings can motivate a new question or plan; they are not automatically misconduct and should not be presented as untouched confirmation.

## Limits and handoff

A written protocol reduces ambiguity but does not remove researcher judgement, measurement error, or all analytic flexibility. Useful exploratory experiments need not each defend a publication claim. Choose the planning detail appropriate to the inference and cost.
