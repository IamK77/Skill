# Keep agreement and later changes attributable

## Use when

Use the task definition, its decision owner, and any change to scope or acceptance.

## Method

Walk representative examples with the responsible person or compare them with an authoritative specification. Record contradictions and the decision that resolves them. Version material changes and trace their impact on implementation, tests, and delivery scope. Preserve the previous decision rather than rewriting history to make a later result appear planned.

## Example and record

If an export requirement changes from current records to historical snapshots, identify the new data and consistency semantics and update acceptance examples. Existing tests may still pass while no longer validating the selected requirement.

## Limits and handoff

Agreement is a human or organisational act; recording a manual checklist item does not create it. Keep unresolved scope visible and do not silently broaden the task because adjacent work seems useful.
