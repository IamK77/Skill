# Elicit examples and constraints from actual work

## Use when

Use the people, workflows, existing artifacts, and authoritative requirements available for the task.

## Method

Ask for a concrete current situation, the desired difference, and an example that would be unacceptable. Use existing screens, logs, support cases, or process documents to make questions specific. Reflect back important decisions and contradictions in plain language. Separate what a stakeholder said from what an observer inferred.

## Example and record

A user asking for a faster report may mean faster first feedback rather than a shorter total computation. Compare those experiences with an example and ask which matters. The answer can change both architecture and acceptance.

## Limits and handoff

Do not manufacture stakeholder consensus or treat one interview as representative of everyone. Keep missing perspectives visible and size further discovery to the decision's consequence.
