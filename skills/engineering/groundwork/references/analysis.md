# Separate the desired outcome from proposed implementation

## Use when

Use the human's request, affected users, current behaviour, and known constraints.

## Method

Identify the outcome, non-goals, interfaces, and decisions that materially change acceptance. Turn vague qualities into questions about observable behaviour or agreed thresholds. Distinguish facts, proposals, assumptions, and unresolved decisions. When a requirement is unknown, ask its owner rather than inventing an industry-normal baseline.

## Example and record

For workspace deletion, clarify reversibility, active jobs, shared resources, and who may initiate it. A retention period is a product decision, not an implementation detail to choose silently. Preserve the answers as concrete success and failure examples.

## Limits and handoff

A small change may need only a short brief. Do not demand an exhaustive analysis framework when the acceptance boundary is already clear, and do not make a speculative technical choice look like the user's requirement.
