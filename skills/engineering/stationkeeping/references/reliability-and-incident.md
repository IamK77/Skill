# Restore useful service and leave an honest incident record

## Use when

Use the observed impact, incident owner, authorised actions, and available service evidence.

## Method

Distinguish the current user impact from hypotheses about its cause. Stabilise or contain with a bounded action, observe the result, and revise the hypothesis as evidence changes. Keep a timeline of decisions, commands, and state so handoffs remain possible. After recovery, identify contributing conditions and concrete follow-up work.

## Example and record

A deployment correlation may justify a safe rollback without proving the deployment caused the incident. Record both the observed recovery and the remaining causal uncertainty. Test the eventual preventive change rather than treating the timeline itself as a regression.

## Limits and handoff

An incident review should support learning and accountable action without substituting blame or a prescribed ceremony for evidence. Recovery claims need representative user-path checks, not only a green dashboard.
