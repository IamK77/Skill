# Names as descriptions, not rankings

## An opening

Imagine the same capability as a shared workshop, independent shops, and a network of exchanged messages. Explore ownership, timing, and change boundaries rather than scoring style names. A mixed structure may express the actual relationships better than a pure example of a named architecture.

## Elaborate a candidate

Use styles to describe relationships among ownership, deployment, communication, and change. A modular monolith, separately deployed services, or event-driven collaboration can each support useful boundaries and each can be poorly implemented. Start from the relevant interactions and operational constraints rather than a maturity ladder of style names.

## A small comparison

Sketch the same capability with one deployment and explicit internal contracts, then with independent deployments. Compare the change and failure scenarios that matter. The contrast may reveal that deployment independence is important for one boundary but unnecessary elsewhere.

Use this note when its question is useful, not as a required inspection. A sketch, a contrast, or an unresolved question can be the result. The [parent lens](../SKILL.md) offers other ways into the subject; execution begins only when a direction and task are chosen.
