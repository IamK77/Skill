# Use words to distinguish concepts

## An opening

Rename the central thing as an observation, a request, a promise, or a result. Does a different word reveal that the program has been combining distinct concepts? Naming can generate a new model rather than merely polish identifiers after the design is fixed.

## Elaborate a candidate

Choose names that reflect the concept, scope, and promise the value actually carries. Distinguish a request from a result, a draft from a saved record, and an observation from an authoritative value where the difference matters. Abbreviations and domain terms depend on the readers and local conventions.

## A small comparison

Renaming cachedUser to observedProfile may expose a freshness assumption; renaming it to profileDraft suggests a different writer and save lifecycle. Let the naming experiment reveal a model question instead of treating terminology as cosmetic cleanup only.

## Keep the choice open

Use this note when its question is useful, not as a required inspection. A sketch, a contrast, or an unresolved question can be the result. The [parent lens](../SKILL.md) offers other ways into the subject; execution begins only when a direction and task are chosen.
