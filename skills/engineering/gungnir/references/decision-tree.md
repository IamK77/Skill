# Choose a bounded security investigation

## Use when

Use a specific hypothesis, an authorised target, allowed techniques, and impact limits.

## Method

Begin with passive local inspection when that can settle reachability or prerequisites. If an active demonstration is necessary and authorised, choose the least invasive test that distinguishes the suspected violation from intended behaviour. Keep an explicit stop condition for sensitive data, availability impact, or scope expansion.

## Example and record

For a suspected cross-tenant read, use permitted test principals and harmless fixtures. Demonstrate the relevant access boundary rather than enumerating unrelated records. Record the exact target revision, prerequisites, input, and result.

## Limits and handoff

A route through this note does not grant authority. Missing scope or uncertain ownership means request clarification, not broaden the investigation.
