# Separate a demonstrated link from a hypothetical chain

## Use when

Use confirmed observations and the additional prerequisites needed for a proposed consequence.

## Method

Describe each link: the capability obtained, the next boundary it could affect, and the conditions that would have to hold. Test only the authorised links needed to establish the finding. Keep possible escalation, deployment assumptions, and untested consequences visibly separate from observed impact.

## Example and record

An unauthorised metadata read may reveal an identifier useful elsewhere, but it does not establish access to that second system. A report can explain the possible connection while stating that the downstream link was not exercised and requires a separate authority and reachability check.

## Limits and handoff

Do not increase impact merely to make a finding more dramatic. Use minimal evidence and do not claim a whole exploit chain from one dangerous-looking primitive.
